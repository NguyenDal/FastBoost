02

Independent work and eligibility

The parties intend to work together on a non-exclusive, independent basis. They do not intend to create employment, a partnership, or an agency relationship. The Provider can accept or decline offered work without a penalty, work for other clients, and choose how, where, and when to work, while meeting agreed deliverables, booked sessions, security requirements, and the law. FastBoost does not promise minimum hours, assignments or income. There are no mandatory shifts or unpaid trial assignments under this agreement.

The Provider must be at least 18 and old enough to enter a contract under local law. They must also be legally allowed to do this work in the place where they are physically working. Identity and skill information must be accurate. Necessary verification must be proportionate; FastBoost will not keep original identity documents or require a security deposit.

The law and how the parties actually work together determine the Provider’s legal status. The title of this agreement, commission pay, and remote work do not decide it. No clause waives mandatory wage, leave, social insurance, tax, termination or other worker protections. If the actual arrangement is employment or requires additional protections, the parties must use a locally compliant arrangement; this agreement does not replace those obligations.

03

Accepting an assignment

Before the Provider accepts a job, FastBoost must give them an Order Brief. The brief must state the order ID, game and region, allowed service and method, starting conditions, target or units, extras, proof of completion, start date or booked session, any deadline, currency, eligible service value, the 70% booster pool, and how approved match counts will be used to split that pool. An individual fee estimate is provisional until all contributions are reviewed. It must define whether wins are total or net, what counts as a qualifying TFT finish, and whether a duo service promises an outcome or only games/time, where relevant.

The Provider accepts a job through a recorded action on FastBoost or a written message. Being assigned a job does not mean they have accepted it. Material changes to scope, conditions, price or timing require agreement before additional work. The Provider must promptly report delays, inaccurate starting information or an inability to finish, and provide accurate progress at agreed milestones. No continuous screen monitoring or access to unrelated private accounts is authorized.

The Provider may suggest a substitute. That person must be approved in writing before receiving access. Approval may be withheld for reasonable skill, identity, customer-consent, privacy or publisher-compliance reasons, not simply to prevent independent working. No sharing of login access with unapproved persons is permitted. The Order Brief must identify participating providers and what counts as a completed match, including how shared sessions are recorded. Match contributions are submitted by each provider and reviewed by an admin under Sections 5 and 7.

04

Publisher rules and prohibited conduct

FastBoost is independent of Riot Games. Riot’s rules prohibit account sharing and certain boosting or rank manipulation. Paying for a duo session does not automatically make it allowed. Neither this agreement nor customer consent changes those rules or guarantees protection from account penalties. See Riot’s Terms of Service and Community Pact. A service label is not evidence that its actual method is allowed.

The Provider must not do work that would break the law or the game publisher’s rules. If they spot a conflict, they must pause the work and tell FastBoost. FastBoost must revise the work to a permitted service or cancel it and account for properly completed work under Section 7; refusing prohibited work is not misconduct. No party may require cheats, scripts, bots, win-trading, intentional throwing, account theft, restriction evasion, falsified results, harassment or misleading “ban-proof” claims.

The Provider must use reasonable care and skill, treat customers respectfully, and avoid promises beyond the accepted brief. A normal loss in a legitimate game is not, by itself, misconduct or grounds for a fine.

05

The 70% fee and eligible service value

For each paid, completed order, FastBoost sets aside 70% of Eligible Service Value as the booster pool. The pool is divided using approved match counts. Eligible Service Value means the customer’s service subtotal after discounts and Gold redemption, before sales tax. Separately identified tips are not included. Gold redemption is deducted once, regardless of how the Gold was earned. A wallet balance funded with money is a payment method, not an additional discount; the original top-up and its later use are never counted twice. Cancelled, partially completed or disputed orders are reconciled separately under Sections 7 and 8.

Routine customer payment-processing fees, advertising, platform overhead and FastBoost’s ordinary chargeback costs are paid from FastBoost’s share, not deducted before calculating 70%. Add-ons count only to the extent allocated to the Provider. Genuine tips expressly intended for that Provider are passed on at 100%, subject to lawful withholding and reversal of that tip if actually refunded. Tips and bonuses are not guaranteed.

FastBoost must disclose the calculation basis and the pool estimate before acceptance. A later promotion, goodwill refund or administrative reclassification cannot reduce an already accepted fee for properly delivered work. Fee corrections for inaccurate information, partial delivery or breach follow Sections 7–8 and require an explanation. There is no entitlement to 70% of the entire order merely because the Provider was briefly assigned to it.

Each Provider submits their completed match count with supporting records. An admin must approve each contribution or return it with a reason. Once all contributions for the order are approved, the Provider’s share equals the booster pool multiplied by their approved matches divided by all approved matches for that order. One Provider receives the whole pool when they have all approved matches. Assignment alone does not earn a share, and an equal split applies only when approved counts are equal.

The pool is rounded to the nearest minor currency unit. Shares are first rounded down, and remaining minor units go to the largest fractional remainders; ties use a consistent account-ID order. Shares always add up to the pool. A zero total or an unresolved contribution leaves the pool unallocated pending review; it does not authorize indefinite non-payment.

06

Payment period, currency and records

Schedule A shows how the Provider will be paid. FastBoost must fill it in before anyone signs. FastBoost initiates manual payment on the 1st of each month for Available earnings through the prior calendar month-end, using America/Winnipeg time. If the selected payment channel cannot initiate transfers on the 1st, FastBoost must arrange initiation by the preceding Business Day. Business Day means Monday to Friday excluding statutory holidays observed in Manitoba. Starting a transfer is not the same as the money reaching the Provider’s account.

Prior-month work verified after the cutoff, including work delayed by FastBoost’s review, is paid within 5 Business Days after verification rather than held for a whole extra month. FastBoost must keep enough funds to meet these dates: its own Stripe settlement schedule or lack of a processor balance is not an automatic extension. There is no minimum withdrawal threshold under the default arrangement.

Record the payment method and verified payee in Schedule A. Earnings and payouts are in USD; do not assume a different currency from the Provider’s residence. FastBoost provides a monthly earnings statement identifying recognized earnings, order references and adjustments. Payment confirmations and any legally required invoice, withholding or transfer records are provided separately. A statement records earnings and is not proof that payment was made. Missing administrative information must be identified promptly and cannot justify withholding unrelated sums.

07

Completion, partial work and reassignment

When work is finished, the Provider must send the evidence agreed in the Order Brief. FastBoost must review each submitted match within 3 Business Days after receiving its completed match count and supporting evidence. Review may finish sooner. An admin must approve the contribution or give a specific, evidenced reason for returning it within that period. Combining matches into a later submission must not extend the deadline for evidence already received. Silence does not automatically approve a match count. If review is overdue, the Provider may escalate it to support@fastboost.gg; FastBoost must promptly resolve the delay and pay undisputed amounts under Section 6. A completion status is not conclusive proof, and a customer’s silence is not a waiver of customer rights. Later genuine issues follow Section 8 rather than erasing the payment record.

For any permitted assignment with a stated rank target, completion requires the target and any LP requirement at handover, not merely at an earlier moment. Other services are measured by the purchased qualifying units or session time in the accepted brief. Completion definitions never override Section 4.

Before gameplay or a booked session actually starts, cancellation normally earns no order fee unless separately paid preparation was agreed. After work starts, properly delivered work remains payable even if the remaining order is cancelled, transferred or stopped for external reasons. The parties must record the value of the delivered portion using the accepted brief and evidence, then apply the 70% pool and approved contribution split to that portion. A cancellation adjustment must not count the same work twice. These cases require a written reconciliation; a completed-order dashboard total alone does not settle them. Undisputed amounts must be paid on time.

If multiple providers contribute, the single 70% pool is divided using their approved match counts under Section 5. A reassigned Provider’s recorded completed contributions must remain part of the review. Stop new work promptly after a cancellation or access-withdrawal notice, record any session already in progress, and transfer necessary progress information through FastBoost. Do not continue working just to increase a cancellation charge.

08

Refunds, chargebacks and payment adjustments

FastBoost handles customer refunds and payment disputes. A refund or chargeback does not automatically mean the Provider owes the full customer price or loses pay from other work. FastBoost bears goodwill refunds, routine processor costs, its own mistakes and genuine third-party payment fraud unless the Provider participated in it. Proven non-delivery or defective work attributable to the Provider may reduce only the corresponding fee, subject to the process below and applicable law.

FastBoost must identify the order, evidence, calculation and proposed remedy in writing and allow at least 7 calendar days to respond. It may temporarily hold only the reasonably disputed amount, must pay all undisputed amounts on schedule, and must release a hold within 30 calendar days of notice unless the parties agree otherwise or a competent authority permits a longer hold. Releasing the hold does not decide the underlying claim. There is no blanket reserve, arbitrary fine or automatic negative balance.

A deduction, repayment or set-off requires the Provider’s written agreement after the facts and amount are known, a binding decision, or clear legal authorization. The parties should consider reasonable correction or reassignment before damages. No double recovery is allowed: customer refunds, fee reversals and damages must account for each other. Wider proven loss is subject to Section 15. The Provider need not complete additional unpaid work to clear a debt.

09

Equipment, taxes and cross-border work

The Provider normally provides their own computer, internet, and workspace and pays ordinary costs of doing the work. Special reimbursable expenses require prior written approval. Each party is responsible for taxes, registration and reporting legally imposed on it. Any service tax the Provider is legally required to charge FastBoost is separately shown on a compliant invoice and is not assumed to be included in the 70% fee.

FastBoost may withhold only amounts legally required, explain the basis, remit them to the correct authority and provide appropriate records. The Provider must supply reasonably necessary tax information securely and notify FastBoost before changing the country or state/province of actual work. The parties must review local employment, tax, privacy and lawful-payment requirements before that change. This agreement does not give immigration or work authorization or make one payment method available in every country.

10

Customer data and account security

For a job they accepted, the Provider may use only the customer details, order instructions, messages, game or session records, and necessary access information FastBoost makes available. Processing is solely to deliver, document and support that assignment on FastBoost’s documented instructions. No data may be sold, used for independent marketing, uploaded to public AI services, or copied to personal cloud storage or unapproved messaging channels.

Use individual accounts, strong unique passwords, available multi-factor authentication, an updated device and encrypted storage for any specifically approved local copy. Limit access to approved persons and to the country of actual work recorded in Section 1, unless FastBoost approves another lawful location in writing before access. Do not request a customer’s email password, full payment details or recovery codes, disable security controls, or change recovery details or spend in-game currency without specific permitted authorization. Customer consent alone does not override Section 4.

Report suspected loss, unauthorized access or disclosure to support@fastboost.gg immediately and no later than 24 hours after awareness. Contain the incident, preserve necessary evidence, cooperate with reasonable investigation and rights requests, and do not conceal it. FastBoost coordinates required customer or regulator notices unless law requires the Provider to act directly. No confidentiality obligation prevents a legally required report.

Access ends when no longer needed, on withdrawal, or on termination. Delete credentials immediately when access ends; return or securely delete other operational copies within 7 calendar days, subject to a documented legal retention duty. Any legally retained copy must be isolated, access-restricted and deleted when that duty ends; retain no credentials for accounting purposes. FastBoost may request reasonable evidence of compliance, not unrestricted inspection of unrelated private files. FastBoost remains responsible for its own privacy obligations.

11

Confidentiality and permitted disclosures

Each party must keep the other party’s private business information safe and use it only for their work under this agreement. This includes customer information, private instructions, access details and genuinely confidential commercial records. The duty excludes information lawfully public, already known without a duty, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source.

Disclosure is allowed to professional advisers bound to confidentiality, to protect or enforce legal rights, to make lawful wage or working-condition disclosures, and to comply with law or report suspected wrongdoing to authorities. Nothing prohibits protected whistleblowing, an honest review or cooperation with a lawful investigation. Ordinary confidential information remains protected for 2 years after termination; trade secrets and personal information remain protected for as long as their nature and applicable law require.

12

Customer communication and no diversion

Keep important order instructions, changes, and proof of completion in FastBoost’s approved channels. During an active FastBoost assignment, do not divert that assignment or its unpaid balance to a private payment arrangement, solicit cancellation to avoid FastBoost’s agreed share, or misuse customer information to solicit unrelated business. Report customer requests for off-platform settlement to FastBoost.

This is not a general non-compete or a post-termination ban on earning a living. It does not prevent work for other clients or competitors, general advertising, independently obtained customers or pre-existing relationships. Confidentiality and the prohibition on misuse of customer data continue as stated above. No recruitment fee, buyout payment or fixed penalty applies merely for leaving FastBoost.

13

Work records and intellectual property

Each party keeps the materials, tools, and know-how they already had before this work. For order-specific reports or other original deliverables created and paid for under a brief, the Provider grants FastBoost a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, copy and adapt them to deliver and support that order, keep records and resolve disputes, and to provide the necessary rights to the customer. This is not a transfer of game, publisher or third-party rights.

FastBoost’s branding and system access are licensed only as needed for accepted work and end on withdrawal or termination. Public use of the Provider’s name, image or private work as an endorsement requires separate permission; customer privacy must also be respected.

14

Pausing and ending the relationship

Either party may stop taking on future assignments by giving 7 calendar days’ written notice. They do not need to give a reason. Any longer notice required by law still applies. The Provider remains free to decline new work during that time. Agree a reasonable handover for accepted work; if it cannot continue, FastBoost may reassign it and must pay the properly delivered portion. The Provider still receives pay they have earned when the relationship ends.

Either party may suspend affected work immediately for credible security risks, illegality, serious misconduct or material breach. For a remediable material breach, give written particulars and 7 calendar days to remedy before termination where reasonably safe; fraud, theft or a serious non-remediable breach may justify immediate termination. The Provider may pause affected new work for overdue undisputed payment after notice. Neither party may hold customer accounts or data hostage.

Game outages, illness or other events outside reasonable control must be reported promptly; agree revised delivery or cancellation without automatic penalties. Payment for delivered work is still due. On termination, FastBoost issues a final statement and initiates undisputed payment within 14 calendar days or by an earlier normal payment date or legal deadline. Sections on accrued payment, data, confidentiality, licences, disputes and responsibility survive as their purpose requires.

15

Responsibility and limits

Each party is responsible for direct loss proven to result from its own breach, negligence, or misconduct. The amount must also account for the other party’s contribution and reasonable steps to reduce the loss. The Provider does not insure FastBoost against every refund, publisher penalty or customer allegation. FastBoost remains responsible to customers for its own contractual obligations. A claimant must give reasonable details and an opportunity to respond; no party can bind the other to a third-party settlement without consent.

To the extent legally permitted, each party’s total ordinary contractual damages under this agreement are capped at the Provider fees paid or payable under it during the 12 months preceding the event, including the affected assignment. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or lost business profits. These limits do not cap unpaid earned fees, approved expenses, required taxes, agreed repayment of an actual overpayment, fraud, deliberate misconduct, gross negligence, breach of privacy or confidentiality duties, or any liability or worker entitlement that law does not allow to be limited.

16

Notices, complaints and governing law

Send formal notices about this agreement to support@fastboost.gg and the Provider email in Section 1. Notice is received when it reaches the designated inbox without a delivery failure; a message arriving after 17:00 in the recipient’s recorded local time is treated as received the next Business Day for contractual notice periods. Actual security and cancellation alerts should also be sent through the available urgent channel. This does not change legal service-of-process or mandatory notice rules.

Raise a dispute with supporting records. The parties will attempt a good-faith resolution within 14 calendar days, but neither must wait to protect a deadline, seek urgent relief or contact a regulator. Mediation is voluntary and requires agreement on cost and venue. There is no mandatory arbitration or class-action waiver.

Subject to mandatory laws applying where the Provider works or otherwise to the relationship, this agreement is governed by Manitoba law and applicable Canadian federal law. Courts and statutory bodies with lawful jurisdiction remain available. There is no exclusive requirement to litigate in Manitoba and no waiver of mandatory local employment, tax, privacy or other protections. Any mandatory local terms or required translation must be identified in a separate document signed by both parties before work begins.

17

Changes, languages and signing

Changes to pay or other commercial terms need written acceptance from both parties. They apply only going forward. A website edit, new customer terms, silence or continuing to use a dashboard does not alone amend this agreement or accepted fees. Reasonable operational security instructions may be updated on notice but cannot rewrite pay or remove rights. If a term is unenforceable, the remainder applies only so far as it can operate fairly and lawfully.

The signed agreement is the English Booster Service Agreement, version FB-BSA-2.2. The Vietnamese view helps people read it. It is not part of the signed agreement. If a translation is needed, provide and review it before signing, and agree in a separate signed writing on its version and how differences will be resolved. Each party must have an opportunity to read the agreement and ask questions in a language they understand. Mandatory local language rights remain unaffected.

The parties may sign matching counterparts or use an agreed lawful electronic-signature method that identifies each signer, the accepted text and the date. Both receive a retainable completed copy. The parties intend to use FastBoost electronic signing for execution of the completed agreement. FastBoost completes the commercial settings before the Provider signs. The Provider signs the saved agreement first, and FastBoost countersigns that same version. Each signer expressly consents to this method and confirms their identity and intent to sign the displayed completed terms. The signed document and its completion record identify the accepted version. Viewing or downloading an unsigned copy does not execute or amend the agreement. Schedule A and the signatures below must relate to the same completed version.
