02

Independent work and eligibility

#

The parties intend a non-exclusive independent service relationship, not employment, partnership or agency. The Provider may accept or decline offered work without a penalty, serve other clients, and choose working methods, place and hours, subject to agreed deliverables, booked sessions, security requirements and law. FastBoost does not promise minimum hours, assignments or income. There are no mandatory shifts or unpaid trial assignments under this agreement.

The Provider must be at least 18, have reached the local age of majority, have contractual capacity, and be legally entitled to perform the services where physically located. Identity and skill information must be accurate. Necessary verification must be proportionate; FastBoost will not keep original identity documents or require a security deposit.

The legal status depends on the actual relationship and applicable law, not this title, commission payment or working remotely. No clause waives mandatory wage, leave, social insurance, tax, termination or other worker protections. If the actual arrangement is employment or requires additional protections, the parties must use a locally compliant arrangement; this agreement does not replace those obligations.

03

Accepting an assignment

#

FastBoost must provide an Order Brief before work is accepted. It records the order ID, game and region, permitted service and method, starting conditions, target or units, extras, delivery evidence, start date or booked session, any deadline, currency, eligible service value and the Provider’s quoted 70% fee. It must define whether wins are total or net, what counts as a qualifying TFT finish, and whether a duo service promises an outcome or only games/time, where relevant.

The Provider accepts through a recorded platform action or written message. Assignment alone is not acceptance. Material changes to scope, conditions, price or timing require agreement before additional work. The Provider must promptly report delays, inaccurate starting information or an inability to finish, and provide accurate progress at agreed milestones. No continuous screen monitoring or access to unrelated private accounts is authorized.

A substitute may be proposed, but must be approved in writing before receiving access. Approval may be withheld for reasonable skill, identity, customer-consent, privacy or publisher-compliance reasons, not simply to prevent independent working. No sharing of login access with unapproved persons is permitted. The Order Brief must identify who earns each allocated portion when multiple providers contribute.

04

Publisher rules and prohibited conduct

#

FastBoost is independent of Riot Games. Riot’s rules prohibit account sharing and prohibited boosting or rank manipulation; paid duo arrangements are not automatically permitted. Neither this agreement nor customer consent changes those rules or guarantees protection from account penalties. See Riot’s Terms of Service and Community Pact. A service label is not evidence that its actual method is allowed.

The Provider must not perform an assignment that requires violating applicable law or publisher rules. Pause and notify FastBoost if a conflict is identified. FastBoost must revise the work to a permitted service or cancel it and account for properly completed work under Section 7; refusing prohibited work is not misconduct. No party may require cheats, scripts, bots, win-trading, intentional throwing, account theft, restriction evasion, falsified results, harassment or misleading “ban-proof” claims.

The Provider must use reasonable care and skill, treat customers respectfully, and avoid promises beyond the accepted brief. A normal loss in a legitimate game is not, by itself, misconduct or grounds for a fine.

Publisher references: Riot Terms of Service · Riot Community Pact

05

The 70% fee and eligible service value

#

For work properly performed by the Provider, the service fee is 70% of the Eligible Service Value allocated to that work. Eligible Service Value is the agreed customer service subtotal after discounts and free promotional Gold/credit, excluding customer taxes, tips and refunded undelivered work. Real-money purchased credit applied to the service counts as payment; the original top-up and its later redemption are never counted twice. A separately identified FastBoost-funded subsidy counts only if stated in the accepted brief.

Routine customer payment-processing fees, advertising, platform overhead and FastBoost’s ordinary chargeback costs are paid from FastBoost’s share, not deducted before calculating 70%. Add-ons count only to the extent allocated to the Provider. Genuine tips expressly intended for that Provider are passed on at 100%, subject to lawful withholding and reversal of that tip if actually refunded. Tips and bonuses are not guaranteed.

FastBoost must display the calculation and quoted fee before acceptance. A later promotion, goodwill refund or administrative reclassification cannot reduce an already accepted fee for properly delivered work. Fee corrections for inaccurate information, partial delivery or breach follow Sections 7–8 and require an explanation. There is no entitlement to 70% of the entire order merely because the Provider was briefly assigned to it.

06

Payment cycle, currency and records

#

The proposed payment settings are in Schedule A and must be reviewed before signing. Under the default schedule, FastBoost initiates manual payment on the 1st of each month for Available earnings through the prior calendar month-end, using America/Winnipeg time. If that date is not a Business Day, payment is initiated on the next Business Day. Business Day means Monday to Friday excluding statutory holidays observed in Manitoba. Payment initiation and bank receipt are different events.

Prior-month work verified after the cutoff, including work delayed by FastBoost’s review, is paid within 5 Business Days after verification rather than held for a whole extra month. FastBoost must keep enough funds to meet these dates: its own Stripe settlement schedule or lack of a processor balance is not an automatic extension. There is no minimum withdrawal threshold under the default arrangement.

The payout method, currency and any conversion rule must be completed in Schedule A. Use a lawful payment channel in the verified payee’s name. No conversion occurs without an agreed rate source and fee treatment. Each statement shows order IDs, eligible value, 70% fee, approved adjustments, tips, required withholding, exchange rate, transfer fees, amount sent and reference. FastBoost provides the statement with payment; a correct provider invoice is supplied where legally required. Missing administrative information must be identified promptly and cannot justify withholding unrelated sums.

07

Completion, partial work and reassignment

#

Submit the agreed evidence when work is finished. FastBoost has 2 Business Days to verify it or identify a specific, evidenced issue. In the absence of such a notice, properly evidenced work is deemed verified for payout purposes. A completion status is not conclusive proof, and a customer’s silence is not a waiver of customer rights. Later genuine issues follow Section 8 rather than erasing the payment record.

For any permitted assignment with a stated rank target, completion requires the target and any LP requirement at handover, not merely at an earlier moment. Other services are measured by the purchased qualifying units or session time in the accepted brief. Completion definitions never override Section 4.

Before gameplay or a booked session actually starts, cancellation normally earns no order fee unless separately paid preparation was agreed. After work starts, properly delivered work is paid at 70% of its original allocated eligible value even if the remaining order is cancelled, transferred or stopped for external reasons. Equally priced units are allocated proportionately; rank segments and differently priced extras use values disclosed in the brief. If an allocation was omitted, the parties must agree a fair evidence-based allocation, with undisputed amounts paid on time.

If multiple providers contribute, each receives 70% of their own accepted allocation, not 70% of the entire order. Stop new work promptly after a cancellation or access-withdrawal notice, record any session already in progress, and transfer necessary progress information through FastBoost. Do not continue working just to increase a cancellation charge.

08

Refunds, chargebacks and payment adjustments

#

FastBoost handles customer refunds and payment disputes. A refund or chargeback does not automatically make the Provider owe the full customer price or lose unrelated earnings. FastBoost bears goodwill refunds, routine processor costs, its own mistakes and genuine third-party payment fraud unless the Provider participated in it. Proven non-delivery or defective work attributable to the Provider may reduce only the corresponding fee, subject to the process below and applicable law.

FastBoost must identify the order, evidence, calculation and proposed remedy in writing and allow at least 7 calendar days to respond. It may temporarily hold only the reasonably disputed amount, must pay all undisputed amounts on schedule, and must release a hold within 30 calendar days of notice unless the parties agree otherwise or a competent authority permits a longer hold. Releasing the hold does not decide the underlying claim. There is no blanket reserve, arbitrary fine or automatic negative balance.

A deduction, repayment or set-off requires the Provider’s written agreement after the facts and amount are known, a binding decision, or clear legal authorization. The parties should consider reasonable correction or reassignment before damages. No double recovery is allowed: customer refunds, fee reversals and damages must account for each other. Wider proven loss is subject to Section 15. The Provider need not complete additional unpaid work to clear a debt.

09

Equipment, taxes and cross-border work

#

The Provider normally supplies their own computer, internet, workspace and ordinary business expenses. Special reimbursable expenses require prior written approval. Each party is responsible for taxes, registration and reporting legally imposed on it. Any service tax the Provider is legally required to charge FastBoost is separately shown on a compliant invoice and is not assumed to be included in the 70% fee.

FastBoost may withhold only amounts legally required, explain the basis, remit them to the correct authority and provide appropriate records. The Provider must supply reasonably necessary tax information securely and notify FastBoost before changing the country or state/province of actual work. The parties must review local employment, tax, privacy and lawful-payment requirements before that change. This agreement does not give immigration or work authorization or make one payment method available in every country.

10

Customer data and account security

#

The Provider may process only the customer identifiers, order instructions, communications, game/session records and strictly necessary access information made available for an accepted assignment. Processing is solely to deliver, document and support that assignment on FastBoost’s documented instructions. No data may be sold, used for independent marketing, uploaded to public AI services, or copied to personal cloud storage or unapproved messaging channels.

Use individual accounts, strong unique passwords, available multi-factor authentication, an updated device and encrypted storage for any specifically approved local copy. Limit access to approved persons and the countries recorded in Schedule A. Do not request a customer’s email password, full payment details or recovery codes, disable security controls, or change recovery details or spend in-game currency without specific permitted authorization. Customer consent alone does not override Section 4.

Report suspected loss, unauthorized access or disclosure to support@fastboost.gg immediately and no later than 24 hours after awareness. Contain the incident, preserve necessary evidence, cooperate with reasonable investigation and rights requests, and do not conceal it. FastBoost coordinates required customer or regulator notices unless law requires the Provider to act directly. No confidentiality obligation prevents a legally required report.

Access ends when no longer needed, on withdrawal, or on termination. Delete credentials immediately when access ends; return or securely delete other operational copies within 7 calendar days, subject to a documented legal retention duty. Any legally retained copy must be isolated, access-restricted and deleted when that duty ends; retain no credentials for accounting purposes. FastBoost may request reasonable evidence of compliance, not unrestricted inspection of unrelated private files. FastBoost remains responsible for its own privacy obligations.

11

Confidentiality and permitted disclosures

#

Each party must protect the other party’s non-public business information received for this relationship and use it only for the agreed purpose. This includes customer information, private instructions, access details and genuinely confidential commercial records. The duty excludes information lawfully public, already known without a duty, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source.

Disclosure is allowed to professional advisers bound to confidentiality, to protect or enforce legal rights, to make lawful wage or working-condition disclosures, and to comply with law or report suspected wrongdoing to authorities. Nothing prohibits protected whistleblowing, an honest review or cooperation with a lawful investigation. Ordinary confidential information remains protected for 2 years after termination; trade secrets and personal information remain protected for as long as their nature and applicable law require.

12

Customer communication and no diversion

#

Keep material order instructions, changes and completion evidence in FastBoost’s approved channels. During an active FastBoost assignment, do not divert that assignment or its unpaid balance to a private payment arrangement, solicit cancellation to avoid FastBoost’s agreed share, or misuse customer information to solicit unrelated business. Report customer requests for off-platform settlement to FastBoost.

This is not a general non-compete or a post-termination ban on earning a living. It does not prevent work for other clients or competitors, general advertising, independently obtained customers or pre-existing relationships. Confidentiality and the prohibition on misuse of customer data continue as stated above. No recruitment fee, buyout payment or fixed penalty applies merely for leaving FastBoost.

13

Work records and intellectual property

#

Each party retains its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how. For order-specific reports or other original deliverables created and paid for under a brief, the Provider grants FastBoost a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, copy and adapt them to deliver and support that order, keep records and resolve disputes, and to provide the necessary rights to the customer. This is not a transfer of game, publisher or third-party rights.

FastBoost’s branding and system access are licensed only as needed for accepted work and end on withdrawal or termination. Public use of the Provider’s name, image or private work as an endorsement requires separate permission; customer privacy must also be respected.

14

Pausing and ending the relationship

#

Either party may end future assignments on 7 calendar days’ written notice without giving a reason, subject to any longer mandatory entitlement. The Provider remains free to decline new work during that time. Agree a reasonable handover for accepted work; if it cannot continue, FastBoost may reassign it and must pay the properly delivered portion. Ending the relationship does not forfeit accrued earnings.

Either party may suspend affected work immediately for credible security risks, illegality, serious misconduct or material breach. For a remediable material breach, give written particulars and 7 calendar days to remedy before termination where reasonably safe; fraud, theft or a serious non-remediable breach may justify immediate termination. The Provider may pause affected new work for overdue undisputed payment after notice. Neither party may hold customer accounts or data hostage.

Game outages, illness or other events outside reasonable control must be reported promptly; agree revised delivery or cancellation without automatic penalties. Payment for delivered work is still due. On termination, FastBoost issues a final statement and initiates undisputed payment within 14 calendar days or by an earlier normal payment date or legal deadline. Sections on accrued payment, data, confidentiality, licences, disputes and responsibility survive as their purpose requires.

15

Responsibility and limits

#

Each party is responsible for proven direct loss caused by its own breach, negligence or misconduct, taking account of the other party’s contribution and reasonable steps to reduce loss. The Provider does not insure FastBoost against every refund, publisher penalty or customer allegation. FastBoost remains responsible to customers for its own contractual obligations. A claimant must give reasonable details and an opportunity to respond; no party can bind the other to a third-party settlement without consent.

To the extent legally permitted, each party’s total ordinary contractual damages under this agreement are capped at the Provider fees paid or payable under it during the 12 months preceding the event, including the affected assignment. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or lost business profits. These limits do not cap unpaid earned fees, approved expenses, required taxes, agreed repayment of an actual overpayment, fraud, deliberate misconduct, gross negligence, breach of privacy or confidentiality duties, or any liability or worker entitlement that law does not allow to be limited.

16

Notices, complaints and governing law

#

Send contractual notices to support@fastboost.gg and the Provider email in Section 1. Notice is received when it reaches the designated inbox without a delivery failure; a message arriving after 17:00 in the recipient’s recorded local time is treated as received the next Business Day for contractual notice periods. Actual security and cancellation alerts should also be sent through the available urgent channel. This does not change legal service-of-process or mandatory notice rules.

Raise a dispute with supporting records. The parties will attempt a good-faith resolution within 14 calendar days, but neither must wait to protect a deadline, seek urgent relief or contact a regulator. Mediation is voluntary and requires agreement on cost and venue. There is no mandatory arbitration or class-action waiver.

Subject to mandatory laws applying where the Provider works or otherwise to the relationship, this agreement is governed by Manitoba law and applicable Canadian federal law. Courts and statutory bodies with lawful jurisdiction remain available. There is no exclusive requirement to litigate in Manitoba and no waiver of mandatory local employment, tax, privacy or other protections. Local review is required before using this draft for a provider in Vietnam, the United States or another jurisdiction.

17

Changes, languages and signing

#

Commercial changes require written acceptance by both parties and apply prospectively. A website edit, new customer terms, silence or continuing to use a dashboard does not alone amend this agreement or accepted fees. Reasonable operational security instructions may be updated on notice but cannot rewrite pay or remove rights. If a term is unenforceable, the remainder applies only so far as it can operate fairly and lawfully.

The English and Vietnamese texts share version FB-PA-1.0 and matching clause numbers and are intended to have the same meaning. The parties select the reference language in Schedule A after having an opportunity to read and ask questions; neither language automatically overrides the other unless selected. A conflict must be raised and clarified in writing, without reducing mandatory rights. A later amendment must be explained in a language the Provider understands.

The parties may sign matching counterparts or use an agreed lawful electronic-signature method that identifies each signer, the accepted text and the date. Both receive a retainable completed copy. This draft webpage, merely viewing it, or typing into a local preview does not by itself execute the agreement. Schedule A and the signatures below must relate to the same completed version.
