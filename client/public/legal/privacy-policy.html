01

About this policy

#

FastBoost is operated by An Nguyen Nguyen, trading as FastBoost, in Manitoba, Canada. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean that operator. We are responsible for the personal information we handle through our website, customer services, and booster management.

This policy accompanies our Terms and Conditions. It explains our handling of information; accepting the terms does not waive your privacy rights or replace separate consent when it is required. Other services you choose to use, including payment and sign-in providers, also explain their practices in their own privacy notices.

02

Information we collect

#
  • Account and profile: email address, username, password hash, verification and sign-in records, account role, and consent choices. You may also provide a display name, photo, biography, Discord handle, country, and birthday.
  • Orders and game details: the service you choose, game name, region, rank, preferences, instructions, progress, assigned boosters, match contributions, and any game credentials needed for the selected service.
  • Payments and rewards: order amounts, currency, discounts, payment references and status, refunds, gold redemption, reward history, coupons, and referral relationships. Payment providers collect billing and payment details during checkout.
  • Communications: contact form details, support and order messages, attachments, message timestamps and read status, and support availability information.
  • Booster records: profile and start date, submitted and approved match counts, calculated earnings, contract recipient details, agreement status, and signed documents where a contract is issued.
  • Technical information: information generated when you connect to the website and its providers, such as IP address, browser information, request times, error records, and session identifiers, used to operate and protect the service.

Information comes from you, your activity on FastBoost, participants in your orders or conversations, and service providers that return payment, sign-in, or contract status. Please provide only information relevant to the service and avoid including another person’s private information without authority.

03

How we use information

#

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify identity, process payments, arrange and deliver orders, manage boosters and agreements, calculate rewards and earnings, and respond to support requests. It also helps us investigate errors, fraud, disputed orders and misuse, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations.

Service communications include verification and password reset emails, order updates, contract notices, and unread-message reminders. Reminders may include a message preview, sender name, and a link to the conversation. Support messages can be reviewed by the support team; order conversations are available to authorized participants and administrators.

Promotional emails have a separate optional choice at signup. You can withdraw that choice by contacting us, or using an unsubscribe option where provided. Withdrawing promotional consent does not prevent necessary account, security, or order communications.

04

Who receives information

#

FastBoost administrators and assigned boosters receive information relevant to their work, such as order instructions, game details, conversation content, and access needed to perform a service. Names and profile images may appear to people participating in your conversations. An uploaded profile image should not contain sensitive documents.

We use providers for hosting, databases, file storage, security, and email delivery, including Render and Amazon Web Services. These providers process information as part of operating the website and delivering communications. The following services are used for specific features:

  • Stripe: payment processing, checkout, fraud prevention, and payment status. FastBoost does not receive or store your full payment-card number or security code. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google or Discord: optional sign-in and account linking. We receive the identity, email, and profile information provided through the approved sign-in flow, not your provider password. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Discord’s Privacy Policy.
  • DocuSign: when a booster contract is sent for electronic signature, recipient name, email, and contract information are sent to DocuSign. Signing creates agreement and audit records, which may include signing times and connection information. See DocuSign’s Privacy Notice.
  • Trustpilot: where review invitations are enabled, completed-order details including customer name, email, order reference, service, and order total are used to arrange an invitation. Publishing a review is optional. See Trustpilot’s Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose relevant information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to establish or defend rights and address fraud or security threats. Any disclosure remains subject to applicable privacy requirements.

05

Game credentials and attachments

#

If your selected service requires game account access, provide credentials only through the designated order fields. Stored game passwords are encrypted, and access is restricted through order permissions. Authorized personnel may need to view them to deliver or support the order.

Do not send email-account passwords, card details, recovery codes, or unrelated credentials in chat or attachments. Change a shared game password once access is no longer needed. Messages and uploaded files become part of the conversation record; sending a file is not a request to make it public.

Contact support if credentials or sensitive information were submitted in error, or if you need access removed. Ending an order does not itself guarantee immediate deletion of all associated records or backups.

06

Cookies and browser storage

#

FastBoost uses browser storage to keep you signed in, remember relevant interface state, cache service information, and handle notifications and chat navigation. Social sign-in uses a short-lived security cookie to connect the sign-in request with the correct browser session. Payment and signing providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies when you use their features.

The “Remember me” option keeps a session available between visits and renews it with account activity. You can sign out or clear browser storage to remove locally saved session information. Blocking cookies or storage may prevent sign-in, checkout, or other features from working correctly.

07

Processing across borders

#

Our providers and authorized service personnel may process information outside your province or country, including outside Canada. Information processed elsewhere may be subject to local laws and lawful access by authorities in those locations. We remain responsible for information under our control and for taking appropriate steps to protect it when others process it for us.

You can contact us to ask about providers and cross-border handling relevant to your account or order.

08

How long information is kept

#

Retention depends on the information and why it is needed. Account information supports your ongoing account; orders, payments, contracts, communications, and contribution records may be needed for service delivery, accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. We aim to retain personal information only as long as needed for those purposes.

There is no single automatic deletion period for all FastBoost records. An account closure or deletion request is reviewed against ongoing orders and necessary recordkeeping. Where information must be retained, we will explain the applicable limitation. Backups and records held by independent providers may follow separate retention schedules.

09

Protecting information

#

We use measures such as password hashing, encryption of stored game passwords, authenticated access, and role and order permissions to protect information. No internet service, email, or storage system can guarantee complete security.

Use a unique FastBoost password, keep your email account secure, and sign out on shared devices. Report suspected unauthorized access or an information exposure promptly to support@fastboost.gg without including passwords or payment-card details in your report.

10

Your choices and privacy requests

#

You can update supported profile fields in your account settings. You may also request access to information we hold about you, correction of inaccurate information, account closure or deletion, or withdrawal of consent by emailing support@fastboost.gg. Tell us which account and request are involved; do not email identity documents unless we arrange a suitable verification method with you.

We may need to verify your identity before acting. Requests are subject to applicable law, protection of other people’s information, and legitimate retention requirements. Withdrawing information needed to operate an account or fulfil an order may limit that service; we will explain the consequences. Optional promotional consent is separate from service access.

We will respond within the periods required by applicable law and explain any permitted refusal or limitation. You may raise unresolved concerns with the relevant privacy regulator, including the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Rights available to you may depend on your location.

11

Age requirements

#

Our Terms require customers creating an account or purchasing services to be at least 18 and to have reached the age of majority where they live. FastBoost is not intended for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support so we can review the account and take appropriate action.

12

Updates and contact

#

We may update this policy as our services and practices change. The version and date appear above. We will provide appropriate notice of significant changes and request additional consent where required; posting an update does not remove your existing privacy rights.

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact An Nguyen Nguyen, trading as FastBoost, at support@fastboost.gg. Please use “Privacy request” in the subject so we can direct your message appropriately.
